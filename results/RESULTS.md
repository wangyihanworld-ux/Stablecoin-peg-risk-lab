Recovery is confirmation of three consecutive observed daily prices within the stated band after the sample minimum. It is not proof of redemption availability.

Daily prices do not capture intraday extremes or executable liquidity. Missing values are never filled. The mark-to-peg shortfall for 10,000 tokens is a hypothetical USD valuation scenario, not realized loss or a guaranteed redemption amount.

Prior-30-day alert thresholds never use the target observation in calibration. These alerts detect observed price deviations; there are no verified incident labels, so accuracy, recall and investment returns are not reported.
